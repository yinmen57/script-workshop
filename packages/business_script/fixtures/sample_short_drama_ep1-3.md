情节梗概: Jamie因前任创伤踏入匿名约会，并在第一次见到Kieran时体验到从未有过的尊重与失控的心动。
[开头Hook First]
My ex had looked at me like my body was an accusation, and that look still followed me into bed long after he was gone.
I’m intersex. That used to feel ordinary. Then Colin found out, and everything soft in me started flinching first.
At one in the morning, I downloaded a queer-friendly app and told myself I was only killing time. Then I saw one photo: a beautiful hand resting on black silk.
Name: Kieran.
Bio: Private. Discreet. Honest. No games.
And one line underneath it that caught in my throat.
Masks optional. Privacy mandatory.
A week later, I stood in a boutique hotel suite wearing a rabbit mask, my palms damp inside the cuffs of my sleeves, listening for footsteps outside the door.
[中间Escalation]
He arrived in a black fox mask and a charcoal suit that looked painfully expensive. He didn’t touch me. He asked if I wanted water. He asked if I wanted the lights lower. He asked questions, then waited for the answers.
When I told him I was intersex, I watched his face for the smallest crack.
Kieran: “May I see you?”
It was careful. Not hungry. Not entitled.
I nodded.
His gaze moved over me, then lifted back to my face.
Kieran: “You’re beautiful.”
The words hit harder because he said them like plain truth.
When he kissed me, he gave me room to pull away. I didn’t. I grabbed his jacket and dragged him closer. On the bed, every touch came with another choice.
Kieran: “Stop, slow, or keep going?”
Jamie: “Keep going.”
Nothing about my body felt like a problem in his hands.
[结尾Cliffhanger]
By morning, my muscles ached pleasantly and sunlight lay across the sheets like evidence.
He handed me water first.
Kieran: “Again?”
My voice came out rough.
Jamie: “Absolutely not. I have work.”
He laughed under his breath. My fingers brushed the red navel piercing under my T-shirt before I could stop myself.
Kieran: “Red suits you.”
Jamie: “You say that like you have opinions about jewelry.”
He leaned in and kissed my temple.
Kieran: “I have opinions about anything on you. See you next time, little rabbit.”
He left, and I sat there with my rabbit mask in my lap, staring at the closed door.
There should not have been a next time.
But the second it clicked shut, I knew I was already in trouble.
剧集 [2]
现身角色: Jamie Reed, Maya, Adrian Blackwood

位置: Vale Creative办公室；会议室

[开头Hook First]
Work the next morning felt like punishment.
Every chair in Vale Creative had apparently been designed by someone who hated the human spine. I had slept three hours, covered the damage with enough concealer to count as engineering, and was trying not to think about a black fox mask when Maya dropped into the seat beside me.
Maya: “Have you heard? New CEO today.”
Jamie: “I hope he’s allergic to meetings.”
She grinned and lowered her voice.
Maya: “Young. Rich. Terrifying. Extremely hot.”
Jamie: “That’s not a job description. That’s a warning label.”
At ten, the whole company packed into the conference room. I was halfway through imagining a quick death under the table when the door opened.
[中间Escalation]
He walked in like a final decision.
Tall. Immaculate suit. Expression carved flat. Shoulders sharp enough to make the room sit straighter on instinct.
Adrian: “Good morning. I’m Adrian Blackwood.”
The name hit the room hard. Chairs shifted. Someone near the back stopped typing.
His voice was wrong.
Not wrong exactly. Just not Kieran’s. Kieran’s voice had warmth buried in it, a low thread of amusement. This man sounded like he reviewed disasters over espresso.
Adrian looked around the room once.
Adrian: “Going forward, I expect punctuality, discretion, and higher standards than this company has apparently been operating under.”
A few people laughed weakly. He didn’t.
Maya bent toward me without taking her eyes off him.
Maya: “He’s awful. I’m obsessed.”
I didn’t answer. I was staring at his mouth, then cursing myself and dragging my gaze higher.
Still, something about him tugged at me in a way I couldn’t place.
[结尾Cliffhanger]
Adrian kept speaking about performance, restructuring, accountability. I should have been taking notes.
Instead, I watched his hands when he turned a page. Long fingers. Controlled movements. No wasted motion.
Then his eyes lifted and landed directly on me.
He didn’t look away.
My stomach tightened for no professional reason at all.
Maya nudged my elbow under the table.
Maya: “Jamie. Stop staring.”
Too late.
Adrian closed the folder in front of him with a quiet snap.
Adrian: “If anyone here has questions, this would be the time.”
The room stayed silent.
His gaze was still on me.
I opened my mouth, not because I had a question, but because I suddenly couldn’t breathe right with him looking at me like that.

[开头Hook First]
I got one drag of my cigarette before a shadow cut across the concrete.
Adrian Blackwood stood over me with that same cold expression from the meeting.
Adrian: “Put it out.”
Smoke caught in my throat. I looked up at him and wished, briefly, for a less humiliating afterlife.
Jamie: “We just met. You could at least pretend to like me first.”
His face didn’t change.
Adrian: “We just finished discussing the no-smoking policy on company grounds.”
Jamie: “I must’ve missed that part.”
His eyes moved over my face once, unreadable and steady.
Adrian: “Clearly.”
[中间Escalation]
He stayed there until I crushed the cigarette beneath my shoe. The silence stretched just long enough to feel deliberate. Then he turned and walked away without another word, polished shoes clean against the stained pavement.
Two minutes later, Maya came through the back exit and stopped short when she saw my face.
Maya: “You look haunted.”
Jamie: “I think our new CEO might actually be the devil.”
Maya considered that seriously.
Maya: “A very expensive devil.”
Jamie: “Exactly.”
I made it through the rest of the workday on caffeine and resentment. By evening, I was hiding in a restroom stall with my phone in my hand instead of finishing my report.
I opened the app before I could talk myself out of it.
Jamie: “New boss. Horrible. Strict. Pretty sure joy dies when he enters a room.”
The typing bubble appeared almost instantly.
Kieran: “Tell me everything, sweetheart.”
[结尾Cliffhanger]
I smiled before I could stop myself, and that should have worried me more than it did.
So I told him about the meeting. The smoking lecture. The voice that could probably freeze moving water. I left out the part where I had stared at Adrian’s mouth like an idiot.
Kieran’s replies came fast, easy, amused in a way I could almost hear.
Kieran: “Is he awful to everyone?”
Jamie: “It’s day one. Give him time.”
Kieran: “And yet you’re messaging me about him instead of sleeping.”
I leaned back against the stall door, biting down on a smile that felt too familiar.
Jamie: “I’m complaining. That’s different.”
Kieran: “Of course, little rabbit.”
The nickname slid under my skin exactly where it always did.
Outside the stall, the restroom door swung open.
A man’s footsteps crossed the tile and stopped near the sinks.
Then Adrian’s voice cut through the room.
Adrian: “Reed. If you’re hiding in here, your report is still due tonight.”
